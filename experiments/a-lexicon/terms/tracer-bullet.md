---
term: tracer bullet
source: Hunt & Thomas, The Pragmatic Programmer
effect: explore
trigger: the task spans layers the agent has not yet seen work together
evidence: untested
---
**Tracer bullet**: one thin, real path fired through every layer before any layer is built out. Salient: the shortest chain that proves the whole system, the integration points along it, and what "it lit up" looks like.
