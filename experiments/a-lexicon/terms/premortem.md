---
term: premortem
source: Gary Klein, "Performing a Project Premortem", HBR 2007
effect: foresee
trigger: the task builds or changes something and no failure story has been told yet
evidence: untested
---
**Premortem**: the change has shipped and failed; the story is written backwards from the wreck. Salient: the single most likely cause, the assumption it rests on, and the cheap check that would have caught it.
